sonic the hedgehog 3

Sonic the Hedgehog 3 is a 2024 film where Sonic, Knuckles, and Tails team up to face a new threat: Shadow, a mysterious hedgehog with unique powers. To stop him and save the planet, they must form an unexpected alliance. The film features Keanu Reeves as the voice of Shadow. It's the third installment in the Sonic movie franchise, following Sonic the Hedgehog (2020) and Sonic the Hedgehog 2 (2022).
